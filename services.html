Services

What we do here.

General practice for dogs, cats, and exotic pets, with the diagnostics and surgical capability to handle most cases without a referral.

Wellness & preventive care

Annual and senior exams, vaccines, parasite prevention, and a plan that fits your pet's age and lifestyle.

  • Annual and twice-yearly senior exams
  • Core and lifestyle vaccines
  • Flea, tick, and heartworm prevention
  • Microchipping
  • Nutrition and weight plans
  • Puppy and kitten packages

When your pet is sick

If something's wrong, we want to see your pet. Call us and we'll find a time, usually the same day.

  • Same-day appointments whenever we can make them
  • Vomiting, diarrhea, and not eating
  • Coughing, sneezing, and breathing changes
  • Itching, ear infections, and skin flare-ups
  • Limping, stiffness, and pain
  • Urinary problems
  • Lumps, bumps, and wounds
  • Ongoing care for chronic conditions: diabetes, kidney disease, arthritis, thyroid

Dentistry

Cleanings, dental X-rays, and extractions under anesthesia, with a monitored recovery and a home-care plan.

  • Oral exams at every wellness visit
  • Full-mouth dental X-rays
  • Scaling and polishing under anesthesia
  • Extractions and oral surgery
  • Home-care coaching

Surgery & laparoscopy

Soft-tissue surgery from routine spays to complex abdominal procedures, in a dedicated surgical suite with continuous monitoring. Minimally invasive laparoscopy for the procedures that benefit most.

  • Minimally invasive laparoscopy: spays (ovariectomy), preventive gastropexy, liver biopsies, and abdominal exploration, with smaller incisions, less pain, and a faster recovery
  • Spay and neuter, including large and giant breeds
  • Mass and tumor removal, with biopsy and pathology
  • Abdominal surgery: bladder stones, foreign bodies, splenectomy
  • Wound repair, lacerations, and abscesses
  • Dedicated surgical suite with continuous monitoring of heart rhythm, blood pressure, oxygen, and breathing under anesthesia
  • Advanced electrosurgery and vessel-sealing for shorter, safer procedures
  • Don't see what your pet needs? Ask. We do far more than fits on this page, and when a case calls for a specialist we'll refer you to one we trust.

In-house laboratory

Bloodwork, urinalysis, and cytology run on site, so most results are back before you leave.

  • Complete blood counts and chemistry panels
  • Urinalysis
  • Fecal and parasite screens
  • Cytology
  • Pre-anesthetic screening
  • Reference-lab send-outs for everything else

Digital imaging

Digital X-ray and ultrasound to see what an exam can't, read the same day.

  • Digital radiography
  • Abdominal ultrasound
  • Endoscopy
    • Flexible upper GI endoscopy
    • Rhinoscopy
    • Cystoscopy
    • Video otoscopy
  • Board-certified radiologist review when needed

Exotic pets

Rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and reptiles are welcome here, not just dogs and cats.

  • Rabbits and guinea pigs
  • Hamsters and other small mammals
  • Reptiles and amphibians
  • Husbandry, housing, and diet reviews

Pharmacy & nutrition

Prescriptions filled in-house, plus diet and supplement guidance for every life stage.

  • In-house pharmacy with most medications on the shelf
  • Prescription diets
  • Supplements and joint support
  • Refills by phone

Call and describe what you're seeing. We'll tell you whether it can wait for an appointment or needs to be seen today.
