Get to know us

We're building Sea Country from the ground up to be the kind of hospital we'd want for our own pets: thoughtful medicine, honest conversations, and a team that remembers your pet's name.

We'll care for pets and their families across Laguna Niguel, Aliso Viejo, Dana Point, Laguna Hills, and Mission Viejo. Whether it's a routine wellness exam, a sick visit, diagnostics, or surgery, you'll always know what we found, what we recommend, and what it costs before anything happens.

Services to keep your pets healthy

Most of what your pet will ever need, in one building, with the diagnostics to answer questions the same day.

View all services

  • Wellness & preventive care

    Annual and senior exams, vaccines, parasite prevention, and a plan that fits your pet's age and lifestyle.

  • When your pet is sick

    If something's wrong, we want to see your pet. Call us and we'll find a time, usually the same day.

  • Dentistry

    Cleanings, dental X-rays, and extractions under anesthesia, with a monitored recovery and a home-care plan.

  • Surgery & laparoscopy

    Soft-tissue surgery from routine spays to complex abdominal procedures, in a dedicated surgical suite with continuous monitoring. Minimally invasive laparoscopy for the procedures that benefit most.

Exotic pets welcome

Rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, and reptiles are seen here alongside dogs and cats, with housing and diet guidance tailored to each species.
